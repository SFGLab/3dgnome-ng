Method
3D-GNOMEᴺᴳ turns chromatin loops and a contact map into an ensemble of 3D structures. It follows the multiscale approach of 3D-GNOME [1, 2]. The loop anchors are placed first and the chromatin between them second. This page follows one job from its inputs to its output.
1. Inputs
A job reads loops, a contact map, gene and enhancer positions, and optionally structural variants. The loops hold the structure together at their anchors. The contact map sets how distance grows with genomic separation and adds contacts between pairs without a loop.

2. Blocks and target distances
Overlapping loops form a block, the computational form of the chromatin contact domains of Tang and colleagues [16] used by 3D-GNOME [1]. The region is split into blocks at points that no loop spans, and stretches without loops have no beads. Every pair of anchors then gets a target distance from one law in bead units, one bead being the distance at 1 kb. A pair without a loop has the target b(s), with s the genomic separation, a power law as in polymer models of chromatin [3, 17]. A loop of strength q has the target d(s, q).
The exponent nu is fitted at load to the contact probability P(s) of the cell type's Hi-C, which falls as s to the power of minus 3 nu [3, 17]. If the fit fails, the run prints the reason and uses 0.285. The strength q of a loop is its PET count n over the typical count of loops of the same span and factor f. The target shrinks as the PET count grows, as in 3D-GNOME [1] and MultiMM [5], and saturates at one bead for a strong loop, figure 1. A Hi-C cell converts to a distance the same way, b(s) times observed over expected contacts to the power of minus one third [3].

3. Anchor phase
All anchors of a chromosome are placed in one fit. The start is a Hilbert curve [4] scaled to b(s), as in MultiMM [5]. L-BFGS-B [6, 7] then minimises the anchor energy below, with L the anchor pairs joined by a loop, B the pairs held at the background and R all other pairs. A loop spring uses k_s when the pair is further apart than its target t and k_q otherwise. Ten annealing runs from perturbed starts end within one percent of the same energy, and L-BFGS-B reaches that minimum. The GPU evaluates the energy and its gradient, the optimiser runs on the CPU, and the fit stops when the relative improvement per iteration drops below 1e-6.

4. Chain phase
The beads between two anchors start on a compact random coil at the bond targets, and the anchors stay where the anchor phase put them. Simulated annealing [8] moves one bead at a time and scores the chain energy below, with C the anchor pairs joined by a CTCF loop and o the direction of the CTCF motif at an anchor. A move that lowers the energy is accepted, and a move that raises it is accepted with the probability below, where E' is the energy after the move. The temperature T falls by a constant factor each step [1, 9]. A wall rejects any move that brings two beads closer than the excluded volume radius.

5. Ensemble and output
The anchor and chain phases repeat from new random starts, one structure per start. Parts of the region with many loops are similar across structures, and parts with few loops vary more. Each structure is saved as an mmCIF file. The job then computes, for every gene, the 3D distance from its promoter to its nearest enhancer within 1 Mb over all structures, as in enhancer3D [10].
6. Engine
A run is a graph of tasks that grows while it runs, figure 3a. The region task adds one task per chromosome, a chromosome task adds its blocks, and each chromosome gets one anchor fit and one chain task per block. Each stage has its own executor, serial, threaded or batched on the GPU. Results are applied in a fixed order, and a run with the same settings and seed gives the same structures.
The chain tasks of many blocks run together in JAX [11] launches on the GPU, figure 3b. Each block is padded to the next size on a fixed ladder, and blocks with the same energy terms are packed into as few launches as GPU memory allows. Each chain has its own random stream, and the grouping of chains into launches does not change the result of a chain. A chain that converges holds its state while the rest of the launch continues. Each annealing step tests 32 proposals against the current state and applies the first accepted one, which keeps the serial Metropolis rule. Excluded volume uses a grid of cells rebuilt every 2,048 proposals. With several GPUs each launch group goes whole to one GPU, and the output does not depend on the number of GPUs.

7. Structural variants
Structural variants change the loops before the anchor phase. The approach comes from Sadowski and colleagues [12], and the rules below extend theirs.
Deletion
Anchors inside the deletion are removed with their loops. Positions after it shift by its length.
Insertion
The inserted sequence is scanned for CTCF motifs. A motif above the score cutoff becomes an anchor and forms a loop with the nearest anchor in convergent orientation.
Inversion
Anchors inside the inversion flip orientation and lose their loops. Each then forms a loop with the nearest anchor in convergent orientation.
8. Validation
A change to the engine becomes the default only after it is measured on GM12878, H1ESC and HFFc6. The measurements are the stratum adjusted correlation with Hi-C [13] at 25 kb, the correlation with Micro-C at 5 kb where a map exists, the exponent of distance against separation, and the number of overlapping beads. A change meant to leave results unchanged is checked by comparing bead coordinates with the previous version. A test against the original 3D-GNOME program compares the distance distributions of both ensembles on a 2 Mb region. The Results page lists the measurements.
9. Limitations
Population average
Loops and contacts are measured over millions of cells. A structure fits that average and is not the conformation of a single cell. Statistics over the ensemble are more reliable than any one structure.
Bead units
Distances are in bead units. One unit is the target distance between beads 1 kb apart. Bead units are not nanometres.
No loops, no beads
Stretches without loops have no beads. Genes there are missing from the distance table.
