3D-GNOME
NG
3D-GNOMEᴺᴳ computes 3D structures of genomic regions up to 20 Mb from CTCF and RNAPII loops, Hi-C contacts and structural variants. Each job returns an ensemble of structures with promoters and enhancers marked, and the 3D distances between them.
